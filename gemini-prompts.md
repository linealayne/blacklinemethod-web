# Blackline Method — Gemini Walkthrough Prompt Library
# /gemini-prompts.md

**Usage instructions:**
Scroll to any frame number. Every entry is fully self-contained.
Copy the prompt text, attach the listed reference images in Gemini, paste, and generate.
Do NOT generate without attaching the listed references — they are the material and lighting anchors.

**Global constraints that apply to all 30 frames:**
- No faces visible anywhere. All human subjects must be in full silhouette, shot from behind, or cropped below the neck.
- Lighting temperature: 2700K warm amber only. No fluorescent. No cool white. No daylight blue except in Frame 27 (skyline view) and Frames 28–30 (clinic, where soft diffused natural light is specified).
- Equipment tier discipline: free weights, plates, barbells → raw old-school iron aesthetic. Cable machines, selectorized, racks → premium matte black with brushed stainless accents.
- No visible brand logos on any equipment, anywhere.
- Rounded corners no greater than 2px on any architectural element.
- No drop shadows. Separation by 1px hairlines or material contrast only.
- Photographic, not illustrative. High-end architectural photography aesthetic.
