# Blackline Method — Spatial Allocation Summary

**Location:** 1418 Dragon Street, Dallas, TX (Design District)  
**Total Square Footage:** 28,000 SQFT (Building footprint: 100 FT wide x 175 FT deep = 17,500 SQFT)  

This document details the scaled-up architectural layout, zoning decisions, and functional rationales for the Blackline Method private members' facility, optimized for an Equinox-Flagship scale (28,000 SQFT total space) with warehouse proportions appropriate for the Design District.

---

## 1. Square Footage Allocation

### Floor 1: Ground Level (17,500 SQFT footprint)
*   **Entry Lobby & Reception:** 1,250 SQFT (4.5% of total)
    *   *Role:* Grand square-shaped entrance lobby (50 FT wide x 25 FT deep) with a custom monolithic stone reception desk, serving as the central membership gateway.
*   **Stair & Elevator Core:** 625 SQFT (2.2% of total)
    *   *Role:* Main vertical circulation lobby featuring a grand architectural staircase and an ADA-compliant glass passenger elevator, situated next to reception for direct second-floor access.
*   **Staff & Service:** 625 SQFT (2.2% of total)
    *   *Role:* Administrative office, dry storage, and facility operations.
*   **Main Training Floor (TRAIN):** 8,500 SQFT (30.4% of total)
    *   *Role:* Core strength space, double-height ceilings, 8 repeating architectural stone arches, massive negative space, wood powerlifting platforms, premium matte black cable systems.
*   **Recovery Wing (RECOVER):** 3,500 SQFT (12.5% of total)
    *   *Role:* Expanded contrast therapy wing: three wood-clad cold plunges, large cedar sauna, separate tiled steam room, dedicated cooldown lounge, and a contrast bench area.
*   **Locker Rooms & Wet Areas:** 3,000 SQFT (10.7% of total)
    *   *Role:* 1,500 SQFT Men's & 1,500 SQFT Women's lockers. Each features raw stone shower wet rooms, changing bays, and quiet lounge seating.

### Floor 2: Mezzanine Level (10,500 SQFT floor plate)
*   **Member Lounge:** 3,500 SQFT (12.5% of total)
    *   *Role:* Social, workspace, and relaxation areas running the full width of the mezzanine edge. Features a glass guardrail overlooking a massive 7,000 SQFT double-height void (which looks down onto the Training Floor and out to the Dallas skyline).
*   **Clinic Wing (HEAL):** 4,000 SQFT (14.3% of total)
    *   *Role:* Serial wellness consults (4 soundproof rooms), central clinical corridor, dedicated multi-chair IV therapy bay, and a lab draw room.
*   **Practitioner Offices:** 2,000 SQFT (7.1% of total)
    *   *Role:* Workspace for staff physicians, physiotherapists, and nutrition experts.
*   **Service & Mechanical:** 1,000 SQFT (3.6% of total)
    *   *Role:* Medical waste storage, linen storage, diagnostic lab refrigeration, HVAC.

## 2. Spatial Layout & Scaling Rationale

### A. Main Training Floor & Arches (8,500 SQFT)
The training floor operates as the double-height volume of the converted warehouse.
*   **Repeating Stone Arches:** Eight pairs of signature stone piers (labeled A1 to A8 from south to north) span a 60-foot clear bay. Spaced at 10-foot intervals, they frame the central circulation lane, providing architectural structure and serving as clear landmarks for walkthrough keyframes.
*   **East-Side Cables:** A dedicated line of premium selectorized and cable equipment is detailed along the East wall, including a dual pulley crossover, lat pulldown with bar attachment, seated row, leg press, and chest press stations.
*   **Powerlifting Platforms:** Three heavy wood-inlay lifting platforms occupy the North edge of the training floor, keeping drops and heavy impact noise near the acoustically isolated locker room walls.

### B. Reception & Front vertical Circulation (1,250 SQFT + 625 SQFT)
To prevent medical clinic visitors or lounge guests from disrupting members on the gym floor:
*   **Direct Access:** The elevator and stairs are integrated directly off the entrance vestibule. Guests arriving for IV therapy or practitioner consults bypass the training floor and locker rooms entirely, going directly up to Floor 2.
*   **Floating Glass Bridge:** On Floor 2, the stairs and elevator tower open onto an enclosed landing in the double-height void. From there, members cross a dramatic 70-foot floating glass-walled bridge suspended over the training floor to enter the Member Lounge, creating a striking transition.

### C. Expanded Recovery Wing (3,500 SQFT)
The recovery wing acts as a sound and physical barrier running horizontally across the building footprint, dividing the noisy Training Floor from the Locker Rooms.
*   **Wet Loop Continuity:** Locker rooms sit directly above the Recovery Wing on the north end of Floor 1. Members can transition from training $\rightarrow$ recovery (plunge/sauna/steam) $\rightarrow$ changing rooms in a circular, logically shielded route.

## 3. Real Buildout Cost Implications
*   **Structural Efficiency:** All heavy plumbing loads (showers, plunges, steam, sauna) are located on the ground floor slab-on-grade.
*   **Suspended Bridge Framing:** The 70ft bridge uses steel truss framing anchored to the main south columns and Floor 2 floor plate, keeping the double-height volume below free of intermediate columns.
